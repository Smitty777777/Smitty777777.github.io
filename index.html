I write high frequency trading software because that is what I am interested in. This is a bookshelf of sorts for that software, excluding the freelance projects I have done for other people and my own trading logic. I have also included some of my academic papers.

I was introduced to programming at 11 via robotC (a C-type language for robotics), and have been writing C# and C++ natively and on the CLR since. I work on very short TTL trading logic, ideally sub-second round trips for strategies that aren't continuous quoting, else I would get quite bored.

In the past I primarily traded calendar and intermarket spreads on the CME and Eurex (bund/bobl/schatz). I now quote illiquid months (back quarterlies) on the CME, Eurex and HKFE.

Why do I do this?
Because it’s comp. Be leet.
