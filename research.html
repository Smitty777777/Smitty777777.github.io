A Continuous Time Markov Model as an Estimator of Future Prices

DOI: 10.6084/m9.figshare.35139007

Abstract

We introduce and expand on existing micro-structure estimators of fair value with a Continuous Time Markov Chain (CTMC) implementation. We compare with mid-price, order-book weighted mid-price, and discrete time Markov estimates on high frequency Nikkei 225 futures data at 0.1, 1, 5, and 10 second forecasting horizons.
